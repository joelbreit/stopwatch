### Privacy Policy for Averaging Stopwatch

**Last updated: January 12, 2026**

**Data Collection**
Averaging Stopwatch does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data.

**Local Storage**
All stopwatch data, including lap times and statistics, is stored locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted to external servers or third parties.

**No Tracking**
We do not use analytics, advertising identifiers, or any form of user tracking.

**No Third-Party Services**
This app does not integrate with any third-party services, APIs, or SDKs that collect data.

**Exported Data**
When you choose to export lap data to CSV, the file is saved locally to your device. You have full control over this data and can choose to share it at your discretion.
